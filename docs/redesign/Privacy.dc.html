Privacy policy
The short version
  • We only use your videos to analyse your tennis.
  • Match and rally videos are deleted right after analysis.
  • The AI coach sees numbers only — never your video.
  • We never sell your data or show ads.
  • Delete your account any time, in the app.

What we collect

Your email address (to sign in), the videos you choose to upload, and the swing and court numbers we compute from them. We also note when you last opened the app.

How we use it

To measure your swing, track your progress over time and write coaching tips. Pose tracking runs on our server; no face recognition is used.

Who processes it

Supabase (sign-in and storage), Google Cloud (analysis server) and Google Gemini (turns your numbers into readable tips).

Your choices

Delete any match or rally from its screen. Delete your account in Profile › Delete account — that removes every video, result and your login permanently.
